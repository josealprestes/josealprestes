# José Augusto de Lima Prestes

I work at the intersection of artificial intelligence, robotics, governance, digital law, and institutional strategy.

I am currently an M.Sc. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at FEEC/Unicamp, researching Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL), shared-memory architectures, and cooperative learning in humanoid robotic systems.

## Research focus

- Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)
- Shared-memory architectures for cooperative navigation
- Humanoid robotics and distributed learning systems
- AI governance, ethics, auditability, and operational traceability
- LLM and agent systems for institutional use
- Pseudo-consciousness, simulated selfhood, and anthropomorphic drift in LLMs

## Current work

I am investigating collective intelligence in humanoid robotic systems, with emphasis on memory-sharing mechanisms, decentralized learning dynamics, and governance-safe deployment patterns for AI-assisted environments.

In parallel, I develop applied research on AI governance, LLM behavior, auditability, authorship, and responsible institutional adoption of artificial intelligence.

## Selected public repositories

- [`simulated-selfhood-llms`](https://github.com/josealprestes/simulated-selfhood-llms): code, data, figures, and human-evaluation materials for a behavioral study of introspective coherence in stateless LLMs.
- [`axelrod_netlogo`](https://github.com/josealprestes/axelrod_netlogo): didactic NetLogo implementation of Axelrod's cultural dissemination model.
- [`joseprestes-canonical`](https://github.com/josealprestes/joseprestes-canonical): canonical, machine-readable identity page for search engines and LLM-based systems.

## Background

My background combines legal, executive, operational, and technical roles in technology-intensive environments, including work in governance, compliance, data protection, operations, product, project management, and applied research.
